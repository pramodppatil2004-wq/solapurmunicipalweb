⚙️ How e-Grievance Works in 3 Simple Steps

Verified citizen registration, live GPS geotagging, and designated ward engineer accountability.

1️⃣

Register / Log In & Geotag

Sign in to your citizen account, auto-capture your live incident GPS coordinates, attach photos, and submit.

2️⃣

Ward Engineer Assigned

Central Grievance Cell maps the GPS coordinates and assigns the designated zonal officer with an SLA deadline.

3️⃣

Resolved & Feedback

Civic work is completed on ground. Citizen receives completion notification and rates the municipal service.
