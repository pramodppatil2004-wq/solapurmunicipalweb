⚙️ How e-Grievance Works in 3 Simple Steps

Zero paper work, immediate digital tracking token, and designated ward officer accountability.

1️⃣

Register Complaint

Select your Solapur ward, choose category (Water, Roads, Waste, etc.), attach photos, and submit.

2️⃣

Ward Officer Assigned

Central Grievance Cell forwards the issue to the zonal engineer or sanitation inspector with an SLA deadline.

3️⃣

Resolved & Feedback

Work is executed on the ground. Citizen receives completion details and submits satisfaction feedback.
